ABOUT HYPERDRIVE

Hyperdrive is an unofficial, fan-made 3D driving tribute inspired by the Tesla Roadster, built with Three.js and TypeScript. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla, Inc.

The car is a procedurally modelled tribute — every panel is generated in code, not a licensed scan. Performance figures (0–100, top speed) are simulated arcade values, and the N boost is a fictional game feature. All audio is synthesized; nothing is recorded from a real vehicle.

Drive the coastal highway, climb the mountain pass, blast through the tunnel, cross the river bridge, cruise the night city — then take it to the test pad and drift.
